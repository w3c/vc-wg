Data Integrity re-structuring overview

manu: This is all credit to Greg Bernstein. When we published DI 1.0 and the suites there was a lot of duplicate text. Greg re-factored all of that and unified. For example, the selective disclosure mechanism is now generalized and available for use by all of the cyphersuites.
… There is also work around single individual hashing and selctive disclosure. There are a number of options to take, hashing together vs hasing individual items. The takewaya is this is good and important work that Greg has done. This will help future cryptosuite authors to re-use these common algotirhms.
… had a discussion around cryptosute identifiers and where certain parameters will go, decided to put them in the identifier string.
… also some BBS optionality stuff has been covered. It's in really good shape now.
